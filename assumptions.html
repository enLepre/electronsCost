Assumptions & method

Cost of supplying one mole of useful electron equivalents.

Chemical route

One mole of reagent costs PM1000 USD and supplies nu moles of useful electron equivalents.

Cchem=PM1000nu
(1)

The factor 1000 converts grams to kilograms. Cchem is in USD (mol e⁻)⁻¹.

Electrical route

The charge and purchased energy required per mole of useful electron equivalents are

Q=FηF
(2)
E=QVηPS=FVηFηPS
(3)

Converting joules to kilowatt-hours gives

Celec=pEκ=pFVκηFηPS
(4)

F = 96,485.33212 C mol⁻¹; κ = 3,600,000 J kWh⁻¹. Thus Celec is in USD (mol e⁻)⁻¹.

Break-even voltage

For p>0, setting Celec=Cchem yields

V∗=κηFηPSpFCchem=κηFηPSPM1000pFnu
(5)
V<V∗⇔Celec<Cchem
(6)

At p = 0, electricity costs zero at every voltage; no unique break-even voltage exists.

Display and scale

Divide molar cost by the Avogadro constant to obtain cost per individual electron equivalent:

ci=CiNA, i ∈ {chem, elec}
(7)

NA = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹. For N moles of useful electron equivalents, total cost is N Ci.

Model assumptions

Prices ≥ 0; V ≥ 0; M, n > 0; 0 < u, ηF, ηPS ≤ 1. Prices and efficiencies are constant over the plotted voltage range.

V is the full operating cell voltage, including losses. The model compares reagent and electricity expenditure for a specified electron transfer; it does not establish reaction feasibility. Other reagents, the paired electrode reaction, equipment, separation and waste costs are excluded.

Reaction basis

O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ → 2 H₂O n = 4; M = 31.9988 g mol⁻¹

2 NH₃ → N₂ + 6 H⁺ + 6 e⁻ n = 3 per NH₃

Workbook chemistry mode restores M = 16 for oxygen and n = 6 for ammonia. Adiponitrile retains an unverified n = 6. Other reaction-specific inputs and all price provenance are listed in Sources; inputs can be edited in Details.
