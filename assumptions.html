Assumptions & method

Cost of supplying one mole of useful electron equivalents.

Chemical route

Let P be reagent price (USD kg⁻¹), M molar mass (g mol⁻¹), n electron equivalents per mole of reagent, and u the useful fraction of those equivalents. The molar electron cost is

Cchem = PM / (1000 n u)
(1)

Here Cchem is in USD (mol e⁻)⁻¹. The factor 1000 converts grams to kilograms.

Electrical route

With Faraday constant F = 96,485.33212 C mol⁻¹ and Faradaic efficiency ηF, the required charge per mole of useful electron equivalents, Q (C mol⁻¹), is

Q = F / ηF
(2)

For full operating cell voltage V (V) and power-supply efficiency ηPS, the purchased energy per mole, E (J mol⁻¹), follows:

E = QV / ηPS = FV / (ηF ηPS)
(3)

Let p denote electricity price (USD kWh⁻¹) and κ = 3,600,000 J kWh⁻¹ the energy conversion factor. The electrical cost per mole of useful electrons is

Celec = pE / κ = pFV / (κ ηF ηPS)
(4)

Celec has the same units as Cchem: USD (mol e⁻)⁻¹.

Break-even voltage

Define V* as the full cell voltage at equal cost. For p > 0, setting Celec = Cchem gives

V* = κ ηF ηPS Cchem / (pF) = κ ηF ηPS PM / (1000 pF n u)
(5)
V < V* ⇔ Celec < Cchem
(6)

At p=0, electricity costs zero at every voltage; there is no unique break-even voltage.

Cost per electron

For route i ∈ {chem, elec}, let Ci be molar cost and ci cost per individual electron equivalent. With Avogadro constant NA = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹,

ci = Ci / NA
(7)

The comparison graph displays ci in USD/e⁻. For N moles of useful electron equivalents, total cost is N Ci.

Model assumptions

P, p, V ≥ 0; M, n > 0; 0 < u, ηF, ηPS ≤ 1. Utilization and efficiencies are fractions (100% = 1), held constant over the voltage range.

V includes cell losses. This cost comparison does not establish reaction feasibility. Other reagents, the paired electrode reaction, equipment, separation and waste costs are excluded.

Reaction basis

O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ → 2 H₂On=4; M=31.9988 g mol⁻¹

2 NH₃ → N₂ + 6 H⁺ + 6 e⁻n=3 per NH₃

Workbook mode restores M=16 for oxygen and n=6 for ammonia. Adiponitrile retains an unverified n=6. See Sources for provenance and Details to edit inputs.
