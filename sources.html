Sources

Inspect the evidence behind each cost.

Published literature, institutional reports and supplier catalogues replace the workbook prices. Each row records the original value, its context and the conversion used by the calculator. Checked 29 October 2026; these are dated benchmarks, not live quotations.

Production costs and LCOE are not purchase prices or delivered tariffs. Ranges use an illustrative arithmetic midpoint. Values retain their original price year, without inflation adjustment. Laboratory catalogue prices are identified separately from literature estimates.

Reading the dataset

Only entries with external cost references are included. Enter your own quotation through Custom on the Details page. Saved comparisons retain their explicit values as user assumptions. Selecting a preset again loads the referenced default.

Supplier prices depend on package, grade and purchase conditions. The calculator does not automatically correct for purity; adjust utilization in Details. Edited or shared inputs are user assumptions and are not verified by the preset reference.

Constants and reaction inputs

The Faraday constant (96,485.33212 C/mol) and Avogadro constant (6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹) follow NIST CODATA. One kWh equals 3,600,000 J by unit definition. Oxygen molar mass (31.9988 g/mol) follows the NIST Chemistry WebBook. Other rounded molar masses remain workbook inputs; this update verifies costs, not every chemistry parameter. Electron counts are reaction assumptions derived by balancing the half-reactions in Assumptions.

Custom starting values are placeholders without external price evidence. The source table always shows dataset defaults, independently of your edits.
